email-tools

A personal, read-only tool that lets its owner read and summarize their own email.

What it does

  • Connects to the owner's own Gmail account through the official Gmail API.
  • Lists recent messages (sender, subject, date, snippet) and reads message text.
  • Uses the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It cannot send, delete, move or modify email.

Who it is for

email-tools is not a public service. It is used only by its developer, on their own accounts. No other users sign in to it.
